🔧 Foreman

A general-contractor AI agent on NEAR. Give it a task + a budget in NEAR — it hires worker agents, negotiates once, checks the work, pays out within budget, and leaves a receipt for every job. NEAR HACKATHON 2026 · Agent Economy track · testnet only.
